**Manual lines** (decision of 24/10/2026, migration 0038: origine « Manuelle » allowed on `bank_transactions` and `accounting_entries`, whose `origine` becomes 10 characters; `services/saisie_manuelle_service.py`): « Ajouter une opération » in the account statement (`ManualOperationModal`, `POST /api/statements/accounts/{id}/operations`, `statements.import`) and « Ajouter une écriture » on `/ecritures` (`ManualEntryModal`, `POST /api/accounting/entries`, `accounting.import`; journal among the account's Sage journals, compte = the account's `compte_comptable`, currency amount required for EUR / USD), both with a mandatory motif and treated **like imported lines** (reconcilable, in lists, totals and fees; a Sage-like manual entry counts as « Sage » for the fee rules, `origine != 'SIMTIS'`). A manual operation goes into the account's « Saisies manuelles » statement (no import batch, `import_repository.releve_manuel`, never in the Journal des imports nor in the « latest import » rule); its balance = the balance of the operation before it (last at its date or earlier, `derniere_operation_au`) + its amount, so it moves the account balance only when it is the latest; later bank balances are untouched. Its line hash is the import's (solde left out when the previous balance was computed), so a bank file or Sage export containing it with exactly the same values sees a duplicate (a Sage export whose account is longer than `compte_comptable` will not match). « Saisie manuelle » badge. One line (imported or manual) is deleted with the bin of its row (`POST /api/statements/transactions/{id}/supprimer`, `POST /api/accounting/entries/{id}/supprimer`, motif), refused when reconciled (code named), pending proposals removed (`suppression_import_service.liberer`); a SIMTIS fee entry keeps its own deletion in « Frais bancaires ». Audits `saisie_manuelle_operation`, `saisie_manuelle_ecriture`, `suppression_operation`, `suppression_ecriture`.

**Reprise of an Excel reconciliation state** (decision of 27/10/2026, migration 0040: origine « Reprise » allowed on `reconciliation_matches`; `services/reprise_service.py`, `repositories/reprise_repository.py`): `python -m app.cli reprise-rapprochement --compte N --banque F --compta F --jusqu-au AAAA-MM-JJ [--appliquer --motif … --email …]` (simulation without `--appliquer`). The two files list what is still **not** reconciled at that date (DATE · LIBELLE · DEBIT · CREDIT, plus N° DE PIECES for the accounting one, first sheet that fits or `--feuille-*`); both are seen from the other side, so DEBIT / CREDIT are inverted; a line without amount is already pointed (not kept); bank lines are found by label without spaces or punctuation (`reprise_service.cle`), same amount, date within 3 days (exact date first); entries by piece + amount, same label preferred, then like the bank. Every other line of the account up to that date (not « Rapprochée », not pending, not replaced) goes into ONE validated N-N match « Reprise » (code, motif as comment, `detail_score.explication`), all or nothing, refused when a line with amount is not found, a line is « Écart » or the reprise does not balance to the cent; a kept line already reconciled or proposed is only reported; pending proposals of the reprise lines are rejected; audit `reprise_rapprochement` (files, counts, totals, kept and reconciled ids). It is cancelled like any match. **`--avec-solde-ouverture`** (30/10/2026): when the statement starts with an opening line that has no entry in front (BMCE « RAPPROCHEMENT (BMCE BLOC) » of 31/12/2017), the account's accounting opening balance (`solde_comptable_ouverture`, its currency amount for EUR / USD, dated at most the state date, else a blocking problem) counts as a Sage debit in the balance (`Bilan.ouverture`, écart = operations + entries − opening); the match is validated with that `ecart_admis` (`reconciliation_service._validate` / `_check_amounts`), its explication and the audit (`solde_ouverture`, `date_solde_ouverture`) name it. Applied in production on 30/10/2026 to BMCE Simtis (…503767) at 30/09/2026 with that option, author Amed, backup `~/backups/simtis-avant-reprise-bmce-2026-10-30-1747.sql.gz`: match BMCE-26-000001 with 3 568 operations and 2 578 entries; 36 operations and 55 entries left « Non rapprochée » (the file's 54 entries plus « AGIOS B.M.C.E 30/09/2026 », 133 159,15, booked after the Excel state, its bank debit expected in October). Since a match can hold thousands of lines, `_views` sends at most `LIGNES_AFFICHEES` (20) lines per side, with `nb_operations` / `nb_ecritures` and the full totals (`CorrespondanceOut`, `nbOperations` / `nbEcritures` in `lib/reconciliation.ts`, « … et N autres écritures » in `MatchPanel`); the lettrage export keeps every line. Applied in production on 27/10/2026 to BP Simtis (…000971) at 30/09/2026, author Amed, backup `~/backups/simtis-avant-reprise-2026-10-27-0920.sql.gz`: match BP-26-000002 with 10 190 operations and 10 112 entries (10 363 118,52 each side), 716 operations and 732 entries left « Non rapprochée »; opening its panel takes about 2.8 s (all its lines are still read to compute the totals).

**Deleting an import** (decision of 23/10/2026, no migration, `services/suppression_import_service.py`): « Supprimer cet import » (bin icon) in the Journal des imports of `/releves` and `/ecritures` (`DeleteImportModal`, mandatory motif) calls `POST /api/statements/{id}/supprimer` (`statements.import`) or `POST /api/accounting/imports/{id}/supprimer` (`accounting.import`); all or nothing, checked before the first write: a line in a **validated** match (SIMTIS fee bookings included) gives 409 naming the codes to cancel first; a statement can be deleted only if it is the account's **latest** import (`import_repository.dernier_releve`, cancelled imports and separate pending-operation files excluded; a pending-operation file can always be deleted), the API sends `StatementOut.raison_non_supprimable` so the button is greyed with the reason; matches citing the lines (pending proposals, rejected, cancelled) are deleted (`reconciliation_repository.matches_touching`, `delete_matches`) and the other lines of a pending proposal go back to « Non rapprochée » (`liberer_lignes`); a statement loses its operations (`delete_transactions`), its balance check and the « Relevé » daily balance it wrote (its `solde` only when a crédit utilisé was entered); entries are deleted with their écarts (`accounting_repository.delete_entries`). The `import_batches` row stays with statut « Annulé » (shown greyed in both journals, `statut`; `/accounting/imports` now lists Confirmé and Annulé), the `bank_statements` row stays empty, and the same file can be imported again (the uniqueness only covers confirmed imports); audit `suppression_import` with file, period, count and motif. After import only Pointage, Lettrage / Escompte and Commentaire change (`PATCH /api/statements/transactions/{id}`, permission `statements.import`, audit `modification_operation`); `GET /api/pointage-types` lists the active types.

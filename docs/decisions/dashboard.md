**P15 Dashboard (done, decision of 14/10/2026, layout of the business mock-up, which replaces the older dashboard spec of the `simtis-design` skill)**: `/dashboard` (`components/dashboard/DashboardView.tsx`) reads `GET /api/dashboard?company_id=&from=&to=` (`dashboard.view`; default the current month in the API, but since 30/10/2026 the screen opens on **1 January of the current year → today** (`periodeRapprochement`, a period in the URL still wins), at most **five years** since 27/10/2026 (`PERIODE_MAX_JOURS` 1827, one year before; dev data: about 1 s for five years), never after today; `services/dashboard_service.py`, reads in `repositories/dashboard_repository.py`, no migration). It reuses the existing rules: balances = `position_banques_service.soldes_par_compte` (statement closing balance, else entered), fees = `reconciliation_repository.frais_bancaire`. Totals cover the company's **MAD current accounts only** (an EUR / USD account only appears in the accounts list, in its currency). Content: 4 KPI (Solde total banques at the end of the period, Encaissements and Décaissements = credits / debits of the period, each with its % variation against the previous period of the same length; À rapprocher = « Non rapprochée » + « À vérifier » operations, link to `/rapprochement`), « Évolution des soldes bancaires » (daily total of the last known balances, Recharts; the « solde prévisionnel » line waits for P14), « Répartition des frais bancaires » (donut by `categorie_frais`: tenue de compte, frais récupérés, agios et intérêts, droits de timbre, commissions et TVA, autres; shares in % from exact cents), « Comptes bancaires » (balance and variation per account, own currency), « Dernières transactions » (5), progress cards Rapprochement (rapprochées / à rapprocher / total) and Écritures comptables (SIMTIS fee entries exported / to export), a « Prévisions (30 jours) » card that says it comes with P14, and computed **alerts** (`dashboard_service.alertes`: line overrun of a MAD account = balance + LIGNE < 0, operations to reconcile, fees to book, fee entries to export, fees up more than 10 %).

**Layout = the business mock-up, exactly** (14/10/2026, replaces a short-lived « cockpit » version; one component per block in `components/dashboard/`): `PeriodePicker` (one pill « 1 oct. 2026 - 14 oct. 2026 » opening the two dates), `KpiTiles` (Solde total banques, Encaissements, Décaissements, Transactions à rapprocher; pastel icon tiles green / blue / red / violet, variation vs the previous period, a `Sparkline` drawn on the real series: daily total balance, `flux` encaissements, `flux` décaissements; the decaissements variation is coloured inversely), `EvolutionCard` (area with dots, « Solde réel » only until P14) and `FraisRepartition` (donut, total in the centre, legend in %), `ComptesCard` (round logo, bank name, number, balance in the account's currency, variation, chevron to `/banques/{bank_id}`) and `DernieresCard` (Date · Libellé · Débit · Crédit · Statut), then `SyntheseCards` (Rapprochement and Écritures comptables rings, Prévisions (30 jours) waiting for P14, Alertes et notifications with their count, 4 shown). Not built because no real data exists: the « Solde prévisionnel » line, the forecast amount, per-card « Ce mois » selects (the period picker covers it) and alert timestamps. The API also returns `ligne_totale` (LIGNE of the MAD current accounts **with a known balance**), `disponible`, per account `bank_id`, `ligne` / `disponible` (null for a currency account), `flux` (every day of the period, 0 without movement, `dashboard_repository.flux_par_jour`) and `a_comptabiliser`; `lib/dashboard.ts` has `jourCourt` and `libellePeriode`. New tokens `violet-bg` / `violet-fg`. The header bell (`components/layout/AlertBell.tsx`) shows the current month's alert count from `GET /api/dashboard/alertes`; **since 21/10/2026** a click opens a panel listing them (coloured dot, message, link to the page, « Voir le dashboard »; closes on Échap, outside click or page change) and the count is reloaded on every page change and every opening (the header stays mounted, so it used to keep its first count); the dashboard's alert card says « Période et banque choisies », since the bell always counts the current month; the global search bar is not built. Measured on the dev data: about 0.1 s for a month.

**Dashboard in two tabs + bank filter** (decision of 19/10/2026, replaces the single-page layout above): `/dashboard` keeps one menu entry and gets two tabs, **Suivi** (alerts, all of them; `TachesCard`: proposals strong / to check, ambiguous operations / entries, fees to book, entries to export, each linking to `/rapprochement`; the Rapprochement and Écritures rings; `BanquesCard`, reconciliation rate per bank; `ActiviteCard`, the 10 latest `/api/audit` actions, shown only with `audit.view`, journal-wide since `audit_logs` has no company) and **Indicateurs** (the 4 KPI tiles, Évolution, the fee donut, Comptes and, for « Toutes les banques », `FraisParBanque` bars). The « Dernières transactions » and « Prévisions (30 jours) » cards are gone. A « Banque » select next to the period sends `bank_id` to `GET /api/dashboard`: every figure (balances, flows, fees, statuses, alerts, tasks, SIMTIS entries) then covers that bank's accounts only; the response adds `bank_id`, `banques_societe` (the select's list), `taches` (`dashboard_repository.propositions` by `seuil_fort`, `ambiguous_transactions` counts, ambiguous entries through `reconciliation_service._ecritures_a_egalite`) and `banques` (`par_banque`, `frais_par_banque`; since 21/10/2026 `par_banque` leaves out pending operations like `statuts`, so the per-bank totals add up to the Rapprochement ring); `/api/dashboard/alertes` skips the tasks (`avec_taches=False`).

**Style refresh of 29/10/2026** (visual only): alerts as coloured rows with icon, bold count and « Traiter → »; tasks as clickable tiles (orange when > 0, greyed at 0); bigger centred rings; activity as a timeline of the **5** latest actions with an icon per action and relative time (`heureRelative` in `lib/dashboard.ts`); KPI tiles of equal height with line-only sparklines on the series' min / max and the variation as a pill; the balance chart's axis fits the series (`bornesAxe`) with a gradient; the fee donut shows each amount in DH next to its %; fee bars show their amount and the bank logo; the accounts card shows 5 accounts and « Voir tous (N) ». Tab, bank and period live in the URL (`?onglet=indicateurs&banque=3&from=&to=`, page wrapped in `Suspense`). Dev data: 0.4 s all banks, 0.15 s for BP.

# Rapprochement : moteur, score, types, groupes, codes

> Texte déplacé tel quel depuis `CLAUDE.md` le 04/11/2026, dans l'ordre d'origine (en anglais, comme ce fichier).
> Un paragraphe plus récent peut en remplacer un plus ancien : chercher « replaces » / « since ».

**P11 (done, spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-06-rapprochement-design.md`)**: 1→1 reconciliation on `/rapprochement` (three panes: Transactions bancaires | Correspondance | Écritures comptables). The engine (`services/reconciliation_scoring.py`, pure `Decimal` rules) only compares a transaction with an entry of the **same bank account**, **opposite sign** (a bank credit is a Sage debit, so `tx.montant == -entry.montant` for an exact amount) and within ± 10 days; score = **montant 50 + date 30 (tolerance 3 days, decreasing: 30 · 22.5 · 15 · 7.5 · 0) + libellé 20** (decision of 07/10/2026, migration 0014, data only; tiers 5 stays in `reconciliation_rules` but `actif = false`; **référence 40 is active again since 08/10/2026** (migration 0020, label « N° chèque / référence »): besides the old rule (piece / reference keys), it scores when the two sides share a number of 5 digits or more read in their labels or references, leading zeros ignored (« CHEQUE N 0173813 » ↔ « EAR1° AXK 173813 », `reconciliation_scoring.numeros`); the total stays capped at 100 and the date tolerance stays 3 days, and a disabled criterion is worth 0 in `reconciliation_service.grille()`; the score detail shows the active criteria plus any that scored under an older grid; pending proposals keep their old score until the engine is run again); proposal from 50, « forte » from **80** (decision of 08/10/2026, migration 0019; 90 before), **ambiguity only when the two best candidates have the same score** (decision of 08/10/2026; before: less than 10 points apart) (**since 16/10/2026** `proposer` assigns pairs from the highest score down: a line already taken gives way to its next candidate, and only a tie at a line's best score makes it ambiguous, with every line linked to it at that score; the ambiguous lists and « Aucune correspondance » keep only the tied candidates, `reconciliation_service.a_egalite`; dev data: ambiguous operations 620 → 228, entries 1 157 → 227, proposals 6 161 → 6 283) (then no proposal, lines « À vérifier »); otherwise the best is proposed and the next one's score is kept in `detail_score.second`: when it is less than `ecart_ambiguite` (10) points below, the API sets `concurrente_proche` and the card shows « Concurrente proche · 2e à N », and such a proposal is never pre-checked even if strong; a pair is proposed only if it is the clear best of both its transaction and its entry.

**Operation types** (decision of 09/10/2026): each label gets a type, Sage `EAR1°` = Chèque, `EAR°` = Effet, `ESP` = Espèces; bank `ENCAISSEMENT CHEQUE` = Chèque, `ENCAISSEMENT EFFET` / `REMISE D'EFFETS` = Effet, `VERSEMENT DEPLACE` = Espèces (`TYPES_SAGE` / `TYPES_BANQUE` in `reconciliation_scoring.py`, prefix of the normalised label); two known and different types are never compared, proposed or matched by hand (409 « Une opération X ne se rapproche pas d'une écriture Y. »).

**Since 28/10/2026 the engine only compares two lines of the same type** (`reconciliation_scoring.types_compatibles`: `type_operation == type_ecriture`, an unknown type « Autre » only with another « Autre »; also in the groups (`reconciliation_groupes`, principal vs entry), the wide search, the candidates and ambiguous lists, and `operations_couvertes`); the manual match keeps its own rule (two known different types refused, « Autre » with a known type accepted); Sage `LCN …` is an Effet (`IMP LCN` stays Impayé); before, an unknown type stayed comparable to anything, so a « CHQ° » entry could face a « VERSEMENT DEPLACE ».

**Currency accounts** (decision of 14/10/2026, migration 0028): the Sage general ledger of an EUR / USD account has one more column, « Devise », the line's amount in the account's currency (débit / crédit stay in MAD). The Sage import reads it (`ImportField` `montant_devise`, headers « Devise », « Montant devise », « Mnt devise ») only for a non-MAD account, gives it the line's direction (a Sage debit is negative, signed or not in the file) and puts the line in error when it is missing (« Montant devise manquant (compte en EUR). »); a MAD account ignores the column. `accounting_entries.montant_devise` (CHECK `devise_meme_sens`: same sign as `montant`). Reconciliation compares the statement (kept in the account's currency) with `reconciliation_service.montant_rapprochement(entry)` = `montant_devise` when present, else `montant`: engine (1→1, groups, through `_ecriture`), balance check at validation, manual match, candidates and the « inclus dans Sage » check. The API sends the account's `devise` with each operation and entry (`bank_account` view-only relationships); the screen shows amounts in that currency instead of « DH » (`unite`, `montantEcriture`, `uniteEcriture` in `lib/reconciliation.ts`), a « Devise » column on the Écritures pane and page, never sums two currencies (the Propositions selection total shows « plusieurs devises »), and the lettrage export has a last column « Montant devise ». Prerequisite in the Comptes screen: the EUR account needs its Sage journal and the USD account its compte comptable.

**Impayés** (decision of 16/10/2026): a fifth type « Impayé », bank labels starting with IMPAYE or IMP (« IMPAYE SUR EFFET ESCOMPTE N 2600009… », « IMP LCN … ») and Sage labels starting with IMPAYE or IMP (« IMP EAR1° … », « IMP EAR° … »); it is compared only with another impayé (or an unknown type), by Sage date against the date d'opération, and joins `TYPES_PAR_NUMERO`, so the same number + same amount is looked up within 180 days. The type filter offers « Impayé » (API `Literal`, `TYPES_OPERATION` in `lib/reconciliation.ts`). **Pending operations « (*) »** (decision of 20/10/2026, migration 0033): a statement line whose label ends with « (*) » (spaces ignored; masked names like « SIM*** » are not concerned, `normalization_service.est_en_cours`) is imported with `bank_transactions.en_cours = true`: it keeps its balance but is never reconciled (left out of `free_transactions`, `candidate_transactions`, `transactions_comparables`, the fee reads; a manual match gives 409), counted apart (`OperationsPageOut.en_cours`, « En cours » counter of `/rapprochement`, badge `EnCoursBadge` in the pane, the statement and the import preview). At a later import, a final line (no « (*) ») **replaces** it when same account, same amount, same label without « (*) » (`libelle_definitif`, case and spaces ignored) and dates at most `JOURS_REMPLACEMENT` (10) days apart, oldest first, once each (`import_service._marquer_en_cours`, recomputed at confirmation, `import_repository.operations_en_cours`; the preview says « remplace l'opération en cours du … », `remplace_date`): the pending row gets `remplacee_par_id` (CHECK `remplacee_si_en_cours`), keeps its balance so the day's balance stays right, disappears from the reconciliation lists and counters (`transactions_query`) and from the dashboard figures (`dashboard_repository`), stays in the statement greyed « Remplacée »; audit `remplacement_en_cours`. The final line is imported and reconciled normally. The migration marked the existing « (*) » operations still « Non rapprochée ».

**Grouped N → 1** (decision of 12/10/2026, `services/reconciliation_groupes.py`, pure rules): after the 1 → 1, the engine proposes groups of several free bank operations for one free Sage entry when their signed sum is exactly the opposite of the entry (same account, dates by the type rules): by shared number (5+ digits) with same-day fees, principal operation + 1 to 3 same-day fees (`famille_frais`: timbre, commission, tva, interet, frais), or same-day fee totals (all stamps of the day = DT D'ENREGISTREMENT, intérêt + TVA = FRAIS D'ESCOMPTE/n°, commission + TVA = COM); score = montant 50 + shared number 40 + date (largest gap) + libellé of the biggest line, capped at 100; another distinct group within `ecart_ambiguite` points makes the entry ambiguous (indistinguishable lines, same labels and amounts, are not); an operation joins one group only; a balanced group wins over a 1 → 1 pair with different amounts; stored as `type = "N-1"` (no migration: `reconciliation_match_items` already holds N items and the partial unique indexes forbid double matching), `detail_score.explication`; never validated by the engine; validation, rejection, cancellation and audit are the existing ones (they already handle N items). The API returns `operations`, `total_operations`, `ecart`, `explication` (`operation` stays the biggest line); `ProposalCard` lists the group lines, `montantBanque` / `operationsDe` in `lib/reconciliation.ts`. Measured on the dev data (2026, Simtis): engine run 5.5 s, 170 groups.

**Manual matches of several lines** (P13, decision of 15/10/2026, no migration: the types `1-N` / `N-N` already existed): `POST /api/reconciliation/matches` also takes `transaction_ids[]` + `ecriture_ids[]` (the old `transaction_id` + `ecriture_id` still works, mixing both forms gives 422); `reconciliation_service.match_manually` checks everything before the first write: 1 to `MANUEL_MAX` (50) lines per side, one bank account, totals exactly opposite (`montant_rapprochement`, so the currency amount for EUR / USD; the 409 names the difference), free lines (a validated match gives 409, pending proposals are rejected), no « Écart » line, one known operation type at most; then a validated match `type_ensemble` (1-1 / 1-N / N-1 / N-N), origine Manuelle, a code, the engine re-run around the lines, audit `rapprochement_manuel` with every line. Score: `score` for 1-1, else the indicative `reconciliation_groupes.score_ensemble` (montant + shared number + date + labels of the biggest lines, capped at 100) with `explication_ensemble` (« 1 opération = 12 000,00 ↔ 3 écritures = 12 000,00 · choisi à la main »). The API sends every entry (`ecritures`, `total_ecritures`; `ecart` uses the currency amount). Screen: « Rapprocher plusieurs lignes » above the panes switches both panes to checkboxes (`multi` prop; a reconciled line cannot be checked) and the centre panel to `PanierPanel` (both sides with their totals, the écart in exact cents, the type, why it is not ready, comment, « Rapprocher (2 → 3) »); pure rules `etatPanier` / `basculerLigne` in `lib/reconciliation.ts`; `ProposalCard`, `MatchPanel` and `HistoryTab` list every entry (`ecrituresDe`, `montantSage`). Not yet: automatic 1 → N proposals (planned as P13.3: 2 to 5 entries, exact sum) and automatic N → N (not planned, manual only).

**Reconciliation codes** (decision of 13/10/2026, migration 0023): every validated match (single, batch, manual, group) gets `reconciliation_matches.code` = « BANQUE-AA-NNNNNN » (e.g. « BP-26-000123 »), one counter per bank account and validation year in `reconciliation_code_counters` (`reconciliation_repository.next_code_number`, `INSERT … ON CONFLICT … RETURNING`, no gap), unique per company (partial index `uq_reconciliation_matches_code`); a proposal has none; a cancelled match keeps its code and a revalidation gets a new one, never reused; the migration backfilled the matches already validated. Shown as « Code » in the Transactions / Écritures panes, the history and the match panel (`CodeBadge`), as « Code rapprochement » on the statement by account, `/ecritures` and as a 12th column after the 11 standard fields of the statement exports; the panes, statement and écritures searches (`q`) also find a code (`ids_with_code`).

**Bank fees are booked in SIMTIS, never matched alone** (decision of 15/10/2026: Mustapha books the bank fees through SIMTIS, no longer by hand in Sage; replaces the fee-only groups and the « alone, same amount within 3 days » coverage described below): `_engine` reads the operations that are bank fees or VAT with the « À comptabiliser » rule (`reconciliation_repository.fee_transaction_ids`, `frais_bancaire()`: pointage or label) and leaves them out of the 1 → 1 step (never proposed alone, never ambiguous; a run resets them to « Non rapprochée », so they show in « À comptabiliser »). Groups keep only an operation principal that is not a fee with 1 to 3 same-day fees, or a shared-number group with at least one non-fee line (`_groupes_possibles(…, frais_ids)`, `grouper(…, frais_ids)`); fee-only groups (all stamps of the day = DT D'ENREGISTREMENT, commission + TVA = COM, intérêt + TVA = FRAIS D'ESCOMPTE) are gone.

**Since the same day, a group is exactly three lines**: an operation principal (not a fee) + one commission + its VAT, same account and same day as the principal, VAT = 10 % of the commission to the cent (`reconciliation_groupes.tva_de_la_commission`, `TAUX_TVA_COMMISSION`, `TOLERANCE_TVA`), sum = the opposite of the entry, every line within `fenetre_jours` of the entry; two commissions without VAT (12 450 − 175 − 25), stamps, shared-number groups or other fees are never grouped automatically (manual match only).

**Screen speed** (15/10/2026, same results, checked on the dev data): `_SommesDuJour.commissions_tva` builds once per account and day the (commission, its 10 % VAT) pairs keyed by their sum, and `_groupes_possibles` only looks up the amount the principal lacks; `operations_couvertes` starts from the days that have such a pair with a fee to check and an index of the day's principals by amount (identical result: 20 covered fees on a year); `candidate_entries` / `candidate_transactions` read the 180-day window only at the opposite amount; `candidates_of_entry` keeps only candidates above the threshold through `_candidat`; the ambiguous lists load the grid and rejected pairs once per page. Year, all accounts: « Frais bancaires » 86 s → 1.9 s, ambiguous operations 4.7 s → 2.2 s, ambiguous entries 6.7 s → 3 s. `operations_couvertes` (« Inclus dans une écriture Sage ») covers a fee only inside such a principal + fees group (the fees to check are never the principal); a fee alone facing a Sage entry of the same amount goes to the SIMTIS booking. A fee can still be matched by hand. Measured on the dev data: ambiguous operations 3 100 → 620, a full run 79 s. Earlier rule: **Fees already inside a Sage entry** (decision of 13/10/2026, no migration): a fee SIMTIS would propose or list (`_frais_a_controler`) that a **free Sage entry** of the same account could already cover is never booked by SIMTIS: `frais_service.frais_inclus` → `reconciliation_groupes.operations_couvertes` (pure) uses the engine's group rules (principal + 1 to 3 same-day fees, shared number, fee totals of a day, even ambiguous) or an exactly opposite amount within 3 days; a rejected pair covers nothing; a fee-only group must fit the entry's family (`FAMILLES_PAR_ECRITURE`: « COM » = commission + TVA, « DT D'ENREGISTREMENT » = timbres, « FRAIS D'ESCOMPTE » = intérêt + TVA; without it 1 + 20 + 1 tied a stamp to a COM of 22). Such fees leave the automatic proposals and « Autres frais », are refused by validation (recomputed) and by manual booking (409 naming the Sage entry), and are listed in a collapsed « Inclus dans une écriture Sage, à rapprocher » section of `FraisCard` (`GET /api/frais` → `frais_inclus`, button « Lancer le rapprochement »). `frais_service.tableau` computes the check once per screen load; only days with a fee to check, their free operations and the Sage entries within `fenetre_jours` of them are read; `type_operation` / `type_ecriture` / `famille_frais` are cached. Measured on the dev data (Simtis, 11 167 operations, 9 761 entries): 0.3 s for a month, 5.6 s for a quarter, about 14 s for a whole year.

**Engine speed at full volume** (15/10/2026, results unchanged): with nothing validated, a whole year for Simtis compared 23 822 free operations with 8 670 entries, 15.3 million pairs in the date windows (1 155 cheques of 10 000 DH against 1 311 EAR1° entries of 10 000 DH alone give 142 000 pairs), and a run took tens of minutes while holding the company lock, so later clicks queued. Now: (1) `reconciliation_scoring._candidat` rejects a pair only when its score is proven below the proposal threshold (points without the label, then the reference, then three upper bounds of the label: common words, lengths as `real_quick_ratio`, `quick_ratio`) and otherwise returns the exact `score`; (2) each line is prepared once (`_Cote`: plain text, words, numbers, keys) and the date gap is computed once per pair (`_ecart_si_comparable`, `_score`); (3) the label ratio uses **`cydifflib`** (new dependency, compiled difflib, the same ratios: 0 difference on 610 000 real label pairs); (4) the groups precompute each operation's numbers and type and inline the date gap; (5) the key regex of `_contient` is compiled once per key. Checked on the dev data: 0 difference between `_candidat` and the full `score` on 381 000 pairs, same group candidates on 1 239 entries. Measured: 1 → 1 290 s → 89 s, groups 70 s → 31 s, a full run (whole year, all accounts, with its writes) 99 s. `run` takes the company lock with `FOR UPDATE NOWAIT` (`_company(sans_attendre=True)`): a second run while one holds the company gets 409 « Un rapprochement ou une validation est déjà en cours … » at once instead of queuing.

**Wide search, up to one year** (decision of 20/10/2026, no migration: the seed adds the rule `FENETRE_ELARGIE`, 365, editable 31–366 in Administration > Paramètres, `parametres_service`, genre « fenetre »): a separate button « Recherche élargie (1 an) » next to « Lancer le rapprochement » (`POST /api/reconciliation/run-elargie`, `reconciliation.validate`, same NOWAIT company lock, audit `rapprochement_elargi`, `reconciliation_service.run_elargi`) looks only at the period's still-free operations (fees and pending operations excluded) and the free entries within ± `fenetre_elargie_jours`; pure rule `reconciliation_scoring.proposer_elargi`: exact opposite amount, same account, compatible types, a shared number (5+ digits or the reference), a date gap **beyond `fenetre_jours`** (inside it the normal engine works) and up to the wide window; entries are indexed by (account, amount), so each operation only looks at its opposite amount (dev data, whole year: 1.2 s, 55 proposals); a pair is proposed only when each side has that single candidate. Stored as ordinary pending 1-1 proposals with `detail_score.elargie` / `ecart_jours`; the normal run never replaces them (filtered out of its `anciennes`), a new wide search replaces only its own. API `CorrespondanceOut.elargie` / `ecart_jours`; `ProposalCard` badge « Écart de N jours », filter « Éloignées » (`ProposalsFilter`), never pre-checked (`defaultSelection`).

**Same-number window**: a cheque or effet with the same number (5+ digits) and the same amount is looked up within `fenetre_numero_jours` (180) instead of ± 10 days.

**Dates compared per type** (decision of 12/10/2026, replaces 09/10/2026; `date_comparee` / `ecart_jours`, `TYPES_PAR_ECHEANCE`): **Chèque and Effet** use the Sage entry date against the bank's date d'opération (points decreasing over 3 days, compared within ± 10 days); **Espèces and Virement** use the Sage **échéance** (else the entry date) against the bank's date de valeur or date d'opération, the closer one, and are also looked up by échéance.

**Virement** is a type since 12/10/2026: label starting with VIREMENT / VIRT / VIR on either side, or RTGS on the bank side. The Simtis Sage export titles the échéance column « ECH », now a synonym and an abbreviation. Earlier wording: an Effet or Espèces uses its Sage **échéance** (else its date) against the bank's date de valeur or date d'opération, the closer one, and is also looked up by its échéance (`free_entries`, `candidate_entries`); every other type uses the Sage date against the bank's **date d'opération only** (the bank shifts the date de valeur). In the dev data no Sage line has an échéance yet, so effets still use their date. Grid and thresholds live in `reconciliation_rules` (reference seeds, editable in the database, **not yet validated by Mustapha**). A proposal sets both lines « À vérifier »; only a human validation sets « Rapprochée » (also for a strong score).

**Espèces: ± 2 days only** (decision of 27/10/2026, no migration: the seed adds the rule `FENETRE_ESPECES`, 2, editable 0–10 in Administration > Paramètres): a pair where either side is Espèces (Sage `ESP…` or bank `VERSEMENT DEPLACE…`; so an `ESP` entry facing a BP versement with a glued label counts) is compared only within `min(fenetre_jours, fenetre_especes_jours)` of the Sage échéance (`reconciliation_scoring.est_especes`, `fenetre_du_type`, `_ecart_si_comparable`; same in the groups and `operations_couvertes`), never in the wide search; beyond it: no proposal, no ambiguity, manual match still allowed; the date points are unchanged. Proposal cards (`ProposalCard`, `AmbiguousCard`, `EcritureAmbigueCard`) show the entry's « Date … · Échéance … » (`datesEcriture` in `lib/reconciliation.ts`).

**Changes of 28/10/2026** (migration 0042, data only): (a) **amount points depend on the cents**: an exactly opposite amount is worth `MONTANT` **60** points when it has cents (1 250,50) and the new rule `MONTANT_ROND` **40** when it is round (1 250,00), 0 otherwise (`reconciliation_scoring.points_montant`, `Grille.montant` / `montant_rond`; used by the 1 → 1 score, `_peut_atteindre`, `_candidat`, the wide search, the N → 1 groups on the entry's amount and `score_ensemble` on the total); both lines are criteria in Administration > Paramètres, thresholds unchanged (50 / 80, total capped at 100); the migration moved `MONTANT` 50 → 60 only when it was still 50, the seeds create `MONTANT_ROND`. (b)

**Espèces are no longer cut at 2 days** (replaces (2d)'s window): they are compared within `fenetre_jours` like the others, and their date points are full up to `FENETRE_ESPECES` days (2, label « Espèces : jours à date pleine autour de l'échéance Sage ») then decrease over `tolerance_jours` (3 days: 22.5 · 15 · 7.5 · 0, `points_date`); they are allowed again in the wide search; `fenetre_du_type` is gone. (b2)

**Espèces date scale fixed in the code** (decision of 30/10/2026, replaces (b)'s points; migration 0043, data only: the rule `FENETRE_ESPECES` is removed, it had been set to 10 days on 28/10/2026 and made every 5 000 DH versement within 10 days score the same date, so near-ties were decided by label noise): an Espèces pair gets 30 points at the exact échéance, 15 at 1 day, 10 from 2 to 5 days, 5 from 6 to 10 days, 0 beyond (`reconciliation_scoring.BAREME_ESPECES`, `points_date`; closest of date de valeur / date d'opération; **no** « sans référence » bonus on the date; 0 when the Date criterion is off); not editable. The ambiguous operation cards now name, for each candidate entry, the other operations at the same score (`AmbiguousView.concurrentes`, `reconciliation_service._concurrentes`, API `CandidatOut.concurrentes`, « Aussi proche de : … · date · score »), the title says « l'écriture hésite avec une autre opération » when there is one candidate; dev data: the ambiguous page 5.0 → 8.4 s. (c)

**Espèces, Virement and Autre score without reference** (same day, no migration: the seed adds the rule `BONUS_SANS_REFERENCE`, 10, a « critère » in Administration > Paramètres, inactive = 0): when both lines have one of these types (`reconciliation_scoring.TYPES_SANS_REFERENCE`, `sans_reference`), `grille_de_la_paire` sets the « N° chèque / référence » points to 0 and adds the bonus to every active criterion (montant 70 / rond 50, date 40, libellé 30; thresholds and windows unchanged, total still capped at 100); applied once in `_score`, `_peut_atteindre`, `_candidat` (its own `paire` grid for the prefilter only), `score_groupe` and `score_ensemble`; Chèque, Effet, Impayé and two different types chosen by hand keep the grid. The wide search looks for these types **by amount and label** instead of a shared number: exact opposite amount, same type, `ratio_libelle` ≥ `SEUIL_LIBELLE_ELARGIE` (0.70), single candidate on each side. Consequences: a same-amount virement within 2 days scores 100 like the same day (cap), so two such candidates tie (ambiguous); « CHEQUE N … » / « CHQ° » payments are « Autre » (no reference points). Dev data (Simtis 2026, free lines only): proposals 19 → 27 (Autre 6 → 13, Espèces 3 → 4, strong Espèces 0 → 3), ambiguous operations 95 → 99. (d)

**A different amount is never proposed** (same day, no migration): `reconciliation_scoring.meme_montant` (exactly opposite, the currency amount for EUR / USD through `_ecriture`) is required by `_candidat`, so by the 1 → 1 engine and the ambiguous entries; in `reconciliation_service` a candidate counts only through `eligible` (score ≥ threshold, not rejected, `Candidate.meme_montant`): `a_egalite` (ambiguous cards, « Aucune correspondance »), `_voisins` (targeted re-run) and the two `_liberer_*_sans_candidate`; so such a pair is neither proposed nor ambiguous, even scoring 50 or more. Groups and the wide search already required the exact amount; the manual match already refuses it; the candidates list of the match panel still shows it as information. The validation guard (409 « Montant différent ») stays for older proposals. Pending proposals keep their old score until the engine is run again; dev data: 6 243 pending 1-1 proposals, strong 5 836 → 5 151 after re-scoring, none below 50.

**A fee alone is proposed against a Sage fee entry** (decision of 05/11/2026, no migration; replaces, for Sage fee entries only, the 15/10/2026 rule « a fee is never proposed alone »): on the server, 17 BP « FRAIS RECUPERES PAR LA BANQUE » (01/08–30/09) were already booked by Mustapha as « INTERET CREDOC/TF… » of the same amount and day, and « INTERETS DEBITEURS » 111 524,95 as « AGIOS BP AU 30/09 », but stayed in « À comptabiliser » (risk of booking them twice). `reconciliation_service.run` adds a pass **3 bis** after the groups (`_frais_seuls`): the fees still free (not in a group) against the Sage entries still free (not proposed, not ambiguous, not in a group, origine ≠ SIMTIS) whose label **starts with** INTERET(S), AGIOS, FRAIS, COM, COMMISSION(S), TVA, TAXE, DT D'ENREGISTREMENT or DROIT(S) DE TIMBRE (`reconciliation_groupes.est_ecriture_de_frais`; « EAP1° CHQ … STE.BIS COM » is not one), same account, exactly opposite amount, within `fenetre_jours` (10, business choice) (`frais_seul_couvert`); `proposer` runs per (account, amount) with the usual grid, ties and rejected pairs, and the pairs are ordinary pending 1-1 proposals, never validated by the engine. A fee facing an entry that is not a fee (cheque, virement…) is still never proposed alone and stays to be booked by SIMTIS. The wide search still ignores fees. Expected on the server: about 18 proposals (BP).

**Nothing after « Au »** (decision of 07/11/2026, no migration; replaces the entries window `au + max(fenetre_jours, fenetre_numero_jours)` of `_moteur` and `au + fenetre_elargie_jours` of `run_elargi`): « Lancer le rapprochement » (1 → 1, N → 1 groups, lone fees) and « Recherche élargie » no longer read any entry dated after the period's end: entries are read from `du − window` up to `au` and kept only when `reconciliation_scoring.jusqu_au` (date of the entry and the compared date, i.e. the Sage due date of an Espèces / Virement, both ≤ « Au »; `reconciliation_service._jusqu_au`). Example: an operation of 28/09 and its entry of 01/10 are not proposed for a period ending 30/09, but are for one ending 01/10 or later (a period covering both dates, or a manual match). Entries before « Du » keep their window; the targeted re-run after a manual match, the manual match and the fee card are unchanged; pending proposals made by a longer period stay until the next run over their period.

# Rapprochement : écran, propositions, ambiguës, lots

> Un paragraphe plus récent peut en remplacer un plus ancien : chercher « replaces » / « since ».

**Lines in proposal stay « Non rapprochée » on screen** (same day, no migration, the database status stays « À vérifier »): the « Non rapprochées » counter of `/rapprochement` adds the « À vérifier » operations (`compteurStatut` in `lib/reconciliation.ts`), the Transactions pane no longer hides them (no `sans_a_verifier`), the Statut filter « Non rapprochée » of both panes and of `/accounting/entries` also reads « À vérifier » (`reconciliation_repository.statuts_du_filtre`), and such a line shows the badge « En proposition » (`PropositionBadge`).

**Sens and dates per pane** (decision of 19/10/2026): the Transactions and Écritures panes also have « Sens » (Tous · Débit · Crédit; bank: debit = money out, credit = money in; Sage: the entry's Débit / Crédit column), sent as `sens` to `/reconciliation/transactions` and `/accounting/entries` (`Literal`, 422 otherwise; `transactions_query` / `entries_query` filter `debit > 0` or `credit > 0`), and their own « Du / Au », which only narrow the screen's period (`bornerPeriode` in `lib/reconciliation.ts`, out-of-period dates are brought back with a message) and travel as the existing `from` / `to`; « Effacer les filtres » resets each pane.

**Type filter** (decision of 14/10/2026): the Transactions and Écritures panes and the Propositions tab have a « Type » select (Chèque · Effet · Espèces · Virement · Autre = no type), sent as `type_operation` to `/reconciliation/transactions`, `/accounting/entries`, `/reconciliation/proposals` and `/reconciliation/ambiguous` (422 for another value). The SQL condition is built from the engine's own prefix lists (`reconciliation_scoring.regex_type` from `TYPES_BANQUE` / `TYPES_SAGE`, `reconciliation_repository.type_condition`: label starting with a prefix as a whole word, punctuation counted as a space), so it never diverges from `type_operation` / `type_ecriture` (test on real labels); proposals are filtered on the type of their main operation; in the Propositions tab the filter also drives its counters.

**Batch validation of the user's selection** (decision of 07/10/2026): `validate-batch` accepts any pending proposal the user checked (**at most 10 000 per call** since 12/10/2026, `LOT_MAX`, 5 000 then 500 before; the screen disables the button above it; measured: 1 513 real proposals in about 6 s, 3 000 test proposals in 15 s, so about 50 s for 10 000, during which the company row stays locked), all or nothing, each one still « Proposée » and balanced (same amount, opposite sign); the screen pre-checks only the strong ones (≥ 80) without a close competitor, a weak one is checked by hand after review, and the audit records `en_lot` and `forte`; reject → « Non rapprochée » and the pair is not proposed again **until the next « Lancer le rapprochement »** (decision of 28/10/2026, no migration: `run` first marks the company's rejections as lifted, `reconciliation_repository.lever_rejets` adds `detail_score.rejet_leve`, the statut stays « Rejetée » for the history, `rejected_pairs` only returns rejections not yet lifted, audit `rapprochement_lance.rejets_leves`; the re-run after a manual match and the wide search do not lift them; « Aucune correspondance » follows the same rule, so an ambiguous line becomes ambiguous again at the next run; the same day the 1 743 rejections already on the server were set to « Annulée » by hand, backups `~/backups/simtis-avant-reouverture-rejets-*`); cancelling a validated match needs a motif; re-running only replaces the engine's pending proposals. A run covers **at most 5 years** (`PERIODE_MAX_JOURS = 1827`, decision of 08/10/2026; 366 days before), a guard against a mistyped year. The engine skips the label comparison of a pair that cannot reach the proposal threshold even with a perfect label (`_peut_atteindre`, same result) and caches normalised labels: a 5-year run on the dev data (2 321 operations, 2 355 entries, 1 million comparable pairs) went from 51 s to 9 s. Manual 1→1 match: same amount, opposite sign, validated at once (`origine` Manuelle), it rejects pending proposals of either line. Migration 0011: `reconciliation_match_items.actif` with partial unique indexes (one active match per transaction and per entry) and `reconciliation_matches.detail_score`. API `/api/reconciliation/*` (run / validate / reject / matches / validate-batch / DELETE need `reconciliation.validate`, reads need `reconciliation.view`); every action locks the company row and is audited. « Écart » is left to P12. `DataTable` now supports `onRowClick` / `isRowSelected` (selected row `bg-simtis-light`).

**Clickable counters and history (07/10/2026)**: the Rapprochées / Non rapprochées counters (`StatButton`) filter the « Transactions bancaires » pane (a second click removes the filter; the pane's Statut filter is controlled by the view).

**« À vérifier » lives only in the « Propositions » tab** (decision of 07/10/2026, **replaced on 28/10/2026**: the lines in proposal are listed again in the panes with a badge « En proposition » and counted in « Non rapprochées »): the pane calls `/transactions?sans_a_verifier=true` (still counted in `par_statut`) and its Statut filter offers Non rapprochée · Rapprochée · Écart; « Propositions en attente » = pending proposals + ambiguous operations (`GET /api/reconciliation/ambiguous`: « À vérifier » without an active match, each with its candidate entries above the proposal threshold, rejected pairs excluded; `AmbiguousCard`, filter « Ambiguës », « Rapprocher » = manual match; since 08/10/2026 it returns the real `total` over the whole filter and 50 operations per `page` (`PAGE_AMBIGUES`; it was silently capped at 100, so the counter never moved), and the counters use that total).

**« Aucune écriture ne correspond »** (decision of 15/10/2026, no migration): an ambiguous card has this button (`reconciliation.validate`); `POST /api/reconciliation/transactions/{id}/aucune-ecriture` (optional `commentaire`, `reconciliation_service.rejeter_ambiguite`) recomputes the operation's candidates above the proposal threshold, records each pair as a **rejected** 1-1 match (inactive items, origine Manuelle, decided by the user: the existing rejection mechanism, so never proposed again; a Sage entry imported later is still compared), sets the operation back to « Non rapprochée » and audits `rejet_ambiguite` with the discarded entries; 409 when the operation already has a match or proposal, or nothing is left to discard.

**Ambiguous entries** (decision of 15/10/2026, no migration): the reverse case, a Sage entry with several equally close operations (« À vérifier » without an active match; they were listed nowhere before), is read by `GET /api/reconciliation/ambiguous-entries` (same filter, 50 per page, `reconciliation_service.list_ambiguous_entries` / `candidates_of_entry`, the symmetric of `candidates`: `repository.candidate_transactions` reads the short window, or the 180-day window only at the entry's opposite amount, since `comparable` admits nothing else beyond it; about 6 s for a page on the dev data).

**Since 16/10/2026 the list keeps only an entry with at least two operations at the same best score** (`_ecritures_a_egalite`, the rule of `candidates_of_entry` + `a_egalite` with the operations read once per account through `reconciliation_repository.transactions_comparables`; `total` and the pages cover these entries only): an entry « À vérifier » only because one operation hesitates between it and another entry has nothing to choose on its side and is handled from « Opérations ambiguës » (dev data: 13 → 0, 0.3 s). `POST /api/reconciliation/entries/{id}/aucune-operation` is the symmetric « Aucune opération ne correspond » (audit `rejet_ambiguite_ecriture`). Screen: the Propositions tab has « Opérations ambiguës » and « Écritures ambiguës » counters; `EcritureAmbigueCard` lists the candidate operations with checkboxes, the checked total and écart (`etatPanier`), and « Rapprocher (N → 1) » through the manual group match (one entry for several operations), plus « Aucune opération ne correspond »; « Propositions en attente » counts both kinds.

**Batch actions in the Propositions tab** (decision of 15/10/2026, only in the « À vérifier », « Opérations ambiguës » and « Écritures ambiguës » filters): « Rejeter la sélection » rejects the checked proposals shown in « À vérifier » (`POST /api/reconciliation/matches/reject-batch`, `ids` up to `LOT_MAX`, all or nothing: each still « Proposée », audit `rejet_rapprochement` with `en_lot`); the ambiguous cards get a selection box, « Tout sélectionner » / « Tout désélectionner » for the page shown and « Aucune correspondance pour la sélection » (`POST /api/reconciliation/transactions/aucune-ecriture` and `/entries/aucune-operation`, `ids` up to `AMBIGUES_LOT_MAX` 500, one company, everything checked before the first write by `_ecarts_operation` / `_ecarts_ecriture`, then the same writes as the single action, audit with `en_lot`). Selections reset when the list changes (page, filter, reload).

**No phantom ambiguous lines** (15/10/2026): a line with no candidate left (all discarded from the other side) is no longer an error for « Aucune correspondance » (single or batch): it simply goes back to « Non rapprochée »; and discarding the candidates of operations (entries) frees each entry (operation) on the other side that was « À vérifier », has no active match and no candidate left (`_liberer_ecritures_sans_candidate` / `_liberer_operations_sans_candidate`, audited as `ecritures_liberees` / `operations_liberees`). Before, such lines stayed in the ambiguous lists with nothing to choose and blocked the all-or-nothing batch; the cards show « Plus aucune … candidate » for the ones left from before.

**A manual match re-runs the engine** on its account around the two lines (± `fenetre_jours`, `reconciliation_service._engine`, shared with `run`), so an operation that competed for the same entry becomes a clear proposal or leaves « Ambiguës »; the audit `rapprochement_manuel` records `relance`.

**Targeted re-run from the ambiguous cards** (16/10/2026): « Rapprocher » on an `AmbiguousCard` / `EcritureAmbigueCard` sends `relance_ciblee: true` (`ManuelIn`, `matchManually` / `matchGroupe` last argument); `reconciliation_service._relance_ciblee` then re-runs only the competing lines instead of the whole account over ± `fenetre_jours` (13 s for one cheque with the 60-day window): the candidates of the matched lines still « À vérifier » without an active match, their own such candidates (the rest of the tie), and the lines whose pending proposal the match rejected; they are compared by `proposer` with their free candidates (a line already proposed elsewhere is never taken back, unlike the full re-run, which can break an unrelated proposal), and only these lines change (proposal, ambiguous, else « Non rapprochée »); audit `relance.ciblee`. The panes and the basket keep the full re-run. Measured on the dev data: 0.35–1.9 s instead of 12.5–14 s; same statuses as the full re-run on every line it touches in 4 of 6 cases, the other 2 being lines the full re-run re-proposed by breaking another client's proposal. Screen: the card is hidden at once (`ProposalsTab` `masqueesOps` / `masqueesEcr`, reset when its list reloads), greyed with « Rapprochement… » on its button while the request runs (`enCours`). The database status « À vérifier » is unchanged (CDC). Sage direction labels come from `sensSage(montant)` in `frontend/lib/reconciliation.ts`: positive = « crédit Sage », negative = « débit Sage » (labels were inverted before 07/10/2026). « Propositions en attente » opens the « Propositions » tab (`ProposalsTab`, `ProposalCard`): one large card per proposal (transaction | score and criteria pills | entry), Valider / Rejeter / Ouvrir dans le rapprochement, « Signaler un écart » instead of Valider when the amounts differ (not selectable), filters Toutes · Fortes · À vérifier, and a sticky bottom bar « N sélectionnées · total » with « Valider la sélection » (confirmation names how many weak ones are included). An « Historique » tab lists every decision (Validée, Rejetée, Annulée) of the period and account, newest first, with who and when (`GET /api/reconciliation/history`, `reconciliation.view`, 50 per page, counts per status over the whole filter), and cancels a validated match (motif required). Migration 0013: `reconciliation_matches.decide_par_id` / `decide_le`, set by every decision (CHECK `decision_tracee`), past decisions taken from `valide_par` / `valide_le` or the audit. No Excel export before P16 (decision of 07/10/2026), **except the lettrage** (decision of 14/10/2026): the « Historique » tab has « Exporter le lettrage » (`GET /api/reconciliation/export?company_id=&bank_account_id=&from=&to=`, `reconciliation.view`, audit `export_lettrage`, period ≤ 5 years): every **validated** match whose operation is in the filter (not only the page, cancelled and rejected excluded; `reconciliation_repository.validated_matches`, `reconciliation_service.export_lettrage`), one Excel row per element grouped by code, bank operations then Sage entries (Code · Côté · Banque · Date · Journal · N° pièce · Libellé · Débit · Crédit · Type · Origine · Score · Validé par · Validé le, Morocco time), matches ordered by their first operation's date, a bold total row; each code balances (bank debit = Sage credit). The button is disabled when the filter has no validated match.

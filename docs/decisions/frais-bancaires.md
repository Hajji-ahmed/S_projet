# Frais bancaires comptabilisés par SIMTIS et « À comptabiliser »

> Texte déplacé tel quel depuis `CLAUDE.md` le 04/11/2026, dans l'ordre d'origine (en anglais, comme ce fichier).
> Un paragraphe plus récent peut en remplacer un plus ancien : chercher « replaces » / « since ».

**Stamp duties booked by SIMTIS** (decision of 13/10/2026, not in the CDC; migration 0024, the one exception to « entries only come from Sage »): the bank's « DROIT(S) DE TIMBRE… » debits of one account and one day make ONE entry « DT D'ENREGISTREMENT » (`services/frais_service.py`, pure `est_timbre` / `regrouper_timbres`; reads in `repositories/frais_repository.py`). Proposals are **computed, never stored** (`GET /api/frais/timbres`, `reconciliation.view`): free stamps only (« Non rapprochée » / « À vérifier », no active match), and a day where Sage already has a « DT D'ENREGISTREMENT » entry for the account is never proposed. `POST /api/frais/timbres/valider` (new permission `accounting.prepare`, given to Administrateur and Comptable by the migration) recomputes on the server, all or nothing, and for each day creates the bank line in `accounting_entries` (`origine = 'SIMTIS'`, journal = `bank_accounts.journal_frais`, else the account's only Sage journal; compte = `compte_comptable`; credit = total) plus a **validated** N → 1 match (`origine = 'Frais'`, `reconciliation_service.create_validated`) whose code is also the entry's N° pièce; audit `comptabilisation_timbres`. `GET /api/frais/export` gives the Sage Excel (Journal · Date · Compte · N° pièce · Libellé · Débit · Crédit: debit `bank_accounts.compte_timbre`, credit the bank account), sets `exportee_le`, `inclure_exportees=true` re-exports; it is refused while `compte_timbre` is empty (to be given by Mustapha). `DELETE /api/frais/ecritures/{id}` (motif) deletes a not-yet-exported SIMTIS entry and its match (audit `suppression_ecriture_frais`, the code is not reused) and frees the stamps; after export it is refused, and the generic match cancellation is always refused for a SIMTIS entry. A Sage import line that is a SIMTIS entry coming back (same account, day and amount, with its N° pièce or the label « DT D'ENREGISTREMENT ») is a « Doublon », never imported twice (`_mark_simtis_entries`). Screen: the « À comptabiliser » counter of `/rapprochement` shows the fee cards above the panes (one card per day, all checked, « Valider la sélection », « Exporter pour Sage », delete).

**Commissions booked by SIMTIS** (decision of 13/10/2026, migration 0025): same mechanism, one entry « COM » per account and day, with **only four exact labels** (`frais_service.type_commission`): « COMMISSION », « TAXE SUR VALEUR AJOUTEE », « COMMISSION EFFET N … REMIS PAR … », « TVA COM EFFET N … REMIS PAR … »; every other commission (VIR. RTGS, REGLEMENT PRELEVEMENT, COTISATIONS CNSS…) stays manual. A day where Sage already has a « COM » entry for the account is never proposed. The export writes one debit line per type (`bank_accounts.compte_commission`, `compte_tva`, `compte_commission_effet`, `compte_tva`) and the bank credit; it is refused while a needed account is empty. The API is per family: `GET /api/frais/{timbres|commissions}`, `POST …/valider`, (an unknown family gives 422), and `DELETE /api/frais/ecritures/{id}` for both; `frais_service.Famille` / `FAMILLES` hold each family's labels, SQL filters and Sage entry label. Screen: `FraisCard` (one per family, « Droits de timbre » then « Commissions », the commission row unfolds its total per type). Dev data: 27 COM days (148 operations, 3 261.50 DH); the other days already have their « COM » in Sage. Escompte + TVA (FRAIS D'ESCOMPTE) is not done.

**General-ledger format and one export** (same day): both fee cards show entries as Sage general-ledger lines (Journal · Date · Compte · N° pièce · Libellé · Débit · Crédit), computed once by `frais_service.lignes_gl` (one debit line per type, the two TVA kept apart, then the bank credit) and returned as `lignes` for proposals and booked entries; stamp duties show **one line per day** on screen (the bank credit line stays in the file, Sage needs it); a missing account shows « à renseigner ».

**One export** for every family: `GET /api/frais/export` (one Excel file, timbres and commissions by date; the per-family exports are gone), one « Exporter pour Sage » button above the cards (`RapprochementView`, count from `FraisCard.onAExporter`).

**One fee table, credits only** (decision of 13/10/2026, option 2, replaces the two cards and the debit lines above): `GET /api/frais` returns both families in one list (each item has `famille`; per day and account, timbres then COM; newest first) and `POST /api/frais/valider` takes `jours[]` with `famille`, all families all or nothing in one transaction; the per-family routes are gone. Every line is **on the credit of the bank account** (`lignes_gl`: one line per type, the two TVA apart, no bank counter-line and no debit): the screen (`FraisCard`, one « Frais bancaires » card) and the Sage export show only these credit lines; Mustapha books the counterpart in Sage, so the charge and VAT accounts are no longer used (columns kept in the database, fields removed from the Comptes form).

**Fees booked by hand** (decision of 13/10/2026, migration 0026): the « Frais bancaires » card also lists « Autres frais à passer à la main » (`GET /api/frais` → `autres_frais`: « À comptabiliser » fees with no active match, **never** a label of an automatic family, `frais_service.est_automatique`: such a line is either proposed or already in Sage's entry of the day, so booking it by hand would count it twice), unchecked by default. Checked operations of **one** account open `ManualFeeModal`: free label (suggestions AGIOS, COM, FRAIS D'ESCOMPTE…, stored in capitals, 60 characters), date (default the last operation's day, between the first operation and today) and « Le montant contient de la TVA »: rate (10 % by default), Frais HT = total ÷ (1 + rate) rounded half-up to the cent and TVA = total − HT (`ventiler_tva`, mirrored by `ventilerTva` in `lib/frais.ts` with `BigInt` cents), both editable, HT + TVA must equal the total. `POST /api/frais/manuelle` (`accounting.prepare`) recomputes the total from the operations, refuses another account, a matched / proposed / non-fee / automatic operation, a zero total, a bad date or a VAT not strictly between 0 and the total, then creates the SIMTIS entry (`famille_frais = 'manuelle'`, `montant_tva`, `taux_tva` in %, NUMERIC(6,2)) and its validated match (audit `comptabilisation_manuelle`). A fee goes to the **credit** of the bank account, a refund (bank credit) to the **debit**; one line without VAT, two lines « Frais » + « TVA » with it (`lignes_manuelle`), in the table and the Sage export; deletion before export as for the others; a Sage line with its N° pièce or the same label is its duplicate. Migration 0026: `accounting_entries.famille_frais` (timbres / commissions / manuelle, backfilled from the label; CHECK: set exactly for SIMTIS entries), `montant_tva` (CHECK: manual only, 0 < TVA < |montant|), `taux_tva` (CHECK: needs a VAT amount, 0 < rate ≤ 100). Dev data: 80 other fees (FRAIS RECUPERES PAR…, FRAIS DE TENUE, AGIOS BANCAIRES, COMMISSION VIR. RTGS…).

**Fees above 200 DH go by hand and are justified to the bank** (decision of 19/10/2026, no migration): the automatic families stay timbres, COM and FRAIS, but in COM and FRAIS an operation of more than `frais_service.SEUIL_MANUEL` (200 DH; 200.00 stays automatic) is left out of the day's proposal (`au_dessus_du_seuil` in `regrouper`, `famille_automatique`, `est_automatique`) and goes to « Autres frais à passer à la main »; stamp duties are never concerned; validation recomputes, so such a fee never enters an automatic entry. « Exporter pour la banque » (`RapprochementView`, `GET /api/frais/justificatif`, `reconciliation.view`, audit `export_frais_banque`, `frais_service.justificatif`, `frais_repository.frais_au_dessus`) downloads an `.xlsx` of the period's fees above 200 DH (« À comptabiliser » rule, stamps excluded, any status): Banque · Compte · Date d'opération · Date de valeur · Libellé · Référence · Montant · Statut (À comptabiliser / Comptabilisé / Rapprochée) · N° pièce SIMTIS · Commentaire, a total per bank and a grand total; nothing is marked, it can be downloaded again. Dev data: 215 such fees (193 not yet reconciled, BP 162).

**Proposals editable or dismissed before validation** (decision of 19/10/2026, migration 0032): in the « À valider » table each proposal has **Modifier** (`EditFraisModal`: entry label, date between the fees' day and today, and its lines: account, label, positive amount, Crédit / Débit, add or remove; credits − debits must equal the operations' total, pure rule `etatLignes` in `lib/frais.ts` with BigInt cents; « Revenir au calcul de SIMTIS ») and **Écarter** (optional motif). Edits stay on screen (`FraisCard` `modifs`, badge « modifiée », reset on reload) and are sent with « Valider la sélection »: `POST /api/frais/valider` `jours[]` take optional `libelle`, `date_ecriture`, `lignes[]` (`frais_service.Modification`, `LigneSaisie`, `_lignes_valides`, all checked before the first write, 409 naming the écart); the piece number follows the chosen date; saved lines go to `frais_lignes` (entry, ordre, compte, libellé, montant > 0, sens; ON DELETE CASCADE) and then replace the computed lines on screen, in the CSV and in the export check (`lignes_ecriture(…, saisies)`, `texte_ligne`); `EcritureFraisOut.modifiee`; the audit records `modifiee`, `date_des_frais` and `lignes_saisies`. A Sage line coming back with a changed label is still recognised by its N° pièce.

**Écarter** (`POST /api/frais/ecarter`, all or nothing, only a day still proposed; `frais_jours_ecartes`: company, account, day, famille, motif, author, unique per account / day / famille; audit `ecartement_frais`): the day is no longer proposed (`_jours` adds it to the excluded days) and its fees go to « Autres frais à passer à la main » (`a_la_main`, `famille_automatique`; manual booking accepts them); a collapsed « Propositions écartées » section lists them with **Reproposer** (`POST /api/frais/reproposer`, audit `reproposition_frais`). Same day: the « À comptabiliser » label rule reads « DROITS? DE TIMBRE » (the plural « DROITS DE TIMBRE SUR REMISE LCN… » was missed, 158 dev operations).

**Booked fees shown like the Sage file** (17/10/2026): the « Comptabilisés par SIMTIS » table of `FraisCard` (`TableauSage` / `EcritureSage`) has the file's columns in its order, Date de pièce · N° pièce · Code journal · N° compte général · N° compte tiers (empty) · Libellé écriture (the exact exported label, `LigneGL.libelle`) · Montant (positive) · Sens, then Export Sage and Supprimer; the 7 optional, always empty columns are not shown and the reconciliation code badge is gone (it stays in the history). The « À valider » proposals keep the Débit / Crédit layout.

**Credit lines only, no bank line** (decision of 17/10/2026, replaces the « complete and balanced » entry below): each fee line goes to the **credit of its own general account** (the accounts of `compte_charge` below), a refund to the debit; there is no bank counter-line any more (`_ligne_banque` removed, `lignes_gl` / `lignes_manuelle`), on screen and in the CSV; `_equilibree` only checks crédits − débits = the entry's amount.

**Fee export, changes of 16/10/2026** (they replace what the next three paragraphs say about the file, the piece number and the credit-only lines): `GET /api/frais/export` returns a **CSV** (`text/csv`, separator « ; », Windows-1252, decimal comma without thousands separator, dates JJ/MM/AAAA, same 15 headers; `frais_service.CSV_SEPARATEUR`, `CSV_ENCODAGE`, `montant_csv`), the « Référence » column stays **empty**, and every entry is **complete and balanced**: one line per type at the **debit of its general account**, then the total at the **credit of the bank account** (`_ligne_banque`, type None, « Banque »; a refund the other way round; `_equilibree` checks débits = crédits and the bank line = the entry's amount). Accounts (`compte_charge`, constants given by the business): timbres and the stamp of FRAIS RECUPERES 61671, COMMISSION / commission effet 61714000, commission RTGS / prélèvement / bancaire / tenue de compte and frais récupérés 61473000 (FRAIS COMMISSIONS BANCAIRE), every VAT line 34552142 (TVA BANQUE 10 %); a manual entry by its label: AGIOS per bank (BMCE 63111000, BP 63112000, AWB 63113000, BMCI 63116000, CIH 63116300), FRAIS D'ESCOMPTE 63117000, FRAIS DE REFINANCEMENT 63119500, COM / COMMISSION 61714000, else 61473000. The bank account fields `compte_timbre`, `compte_commission`… are no longer used. `lignes_gl` / `lignes_manuelle` return these lines to the screen too (`FraisCard` shows the bank line « … · Banque »).

**N° pièce BANQUE AAMMNNN** (« BP2610123 », `numero_piece(banque, jour, n)`): one counter per company, **bank** and month (`frais_piece_counters.bank_id`, migration 0031: table rebuilt, not-yet-exported SIMTIS entries renumbered by date with audit `renumerotation_piece`, dev: 100; exported ones keep their number).

**Sage import format of the fee export** (decision of 15/10/2026, replaces the 7 columns Journal · Date · Compte · N° pièce · Libellé · Débit · Crédit): `GET /api/frais/export` writes the 15 columns of Sage's « import paramétrable » in this order (`frais_service.EXPORT_HEADERS`): * Date de pièce · N° pièce · * Code journal · * N° compte général · N° compte tiers · Libellé écriture · Montant · Sens · Mode de règlement · Date d'échéance · Référence · Numéro facture · Montant devise · Cours de la devise · Code ISO devise; still `.xlsx`. Montant is always positive and Sens is « Crédit » for a fee, « Débit » for a refund (`SENS_CREDIT` / `SENS_DEBIT`); the optional columns stay empty. An entry of an EUR / USD account is refused (409 naming the account) because Sage needs the MAD amount, the currency amount and the rate, and SIMTIS has no rate yet. The screen (`FraisCard`) keeps its Débit / Crédit preview.

**N° pièce AAMMNNN** (decision of 15/10/2026, migration 0030): a SIMTIS fee entry's `numero_piece` is the year and month of its entry date plus a 3-digit rank of the month, e.g. « 2610018 » (`frais_service.numero_piece`, `PIECE_MAX` 999, refused above), given at booking (automatic or manual) from one counter per company and month (`frais_piece_counters`, `frais_repository.next_piece_number`, `INSERT … ON CONFLICT … RETURNING`, no gap); it was the reconciliation code before. The reconciliation code goes to the export's « Référence » column; the API's `codes` after a validation are these piece numbers, `EcritureFraisOut` sends both `code` and `numero_piece`, and the card shows the piece with the code badge under it. The migration renumbered the not-yet-exported SIMTIS entries by date (audit `renumerotation_piece`, dev: 4) and seeded the counters; exported ones keep their old number, which Sage knows.

**VAT line labels** (decision of 14/10/2026): in the Sage export and on screen, a VAT line of a SIMTIS entry is labelled « TVA » + the entry's label (« COM » → « TVA COM », « FRAIS » → « TVA FRAIS », a manual « AGIOS » → « TVA AGIOS »), other lines keep the entry's label (`frais_service.est_ligne_tva` / `libelle_ligne`, sent as `LigneGLOut.libelle`, shown by `FraisCard`); DT D'ENREGISTREMENT is unchanged.

**Fees with VAT inside go to the entry named by the label's first word** (decision of 14/10/2026, replaces the next paragraph's grouping): COMMISSION VIR. RTGS…, COMMISSION REGLEMENT PRELEVEMENT…, COMMISSION BANCAIRE and COMMISSION TENUE DE COMPTE belong to the **« COM »** family (`type_commission`, after the four exact labels, `_COMMISSIONS_TVA`), only FRAIS RECUPERES PAR LA BANQUE stays in **« FRAIS »** (`type_frais_tva`, `_FRAIS_RECUPERES`, `LIBELLE_FRAIS_SQL`); the VAT split (`ventilation`) is unchanged, so a COM entry may show « Commission RTGS » + « TVA commission RTGS » next to « Commission » and « TVA de la commission »; `FAMILLES_PAR_ECRITURE` « FRAIS » = frais + TVA + timbre.

**Fees with VAT inside, booked automatically** (decision of 14/10/2026, migration 0027): a third automatic family `frais` (`frais_service.FRAIS_TVA`, entry label « FRAIS », one per account and day, a day where Sage already has a « FRAIS » entry is never proposed) takes the **debits** labelled COMMISSION VIR. RTGS…, COMMISSION REGLEMENT PRELEVEMENT…, COMMISSION BANCAIRE, COMMISSION TENUE DE COMPTE and FRAIS RECUPERES PAR LA BANQUE (`type_frais_tva`, punctuation and accents ignored). Each operation is split by `ventilation`: HT = amount ÷ 1.10 rounded half-up to the cent, TVA = the rest, and a FRAIS RECUPERES of exactly 77.50 = stamp 50 + 25 + TVA 2.50; `details_par_type` sums the parts, so the entry shows one line per part (« Commission RTGS », « TVA commission RTGS », « Timbre frais récupérés »…, all on the bank's credit), in the table and the Sage export. A refund (credit) is never automatic (`est_automatique(libelle, montant)`): it stays in « Autres frais ». Everything else (AGIOS, INTERETS DEBITEURS, DROITS DE GARDE, REMBOURSEMENT FRAIS, FRAIS DE TENUE DE COMPTE…) stays manual. `FAMILLES_PAR_ECRITURE` lets a Sage « FRAIS » entry cover commission, frais, TVA and timbre lines; `_ECRITURES_SIMTIS` recognises a SIMTIS « FRAIS » entry coming back from Sage. Migration 0027 only widens the CHECK `famille_frais` to « frais ». Dev data (Simtis, 2026): 28 FRAIS days, 49 operations, 85 953.57 DH; 29 other fees left to book by hand.

**À comptabiliser** (decision of 12/10/2026) is a view, not a status: bank fees still « Non rapprochée » (pointage COM, DROIT, TVA, FRAIS, AGIOS, AGIOS D'ECHELLE, INTERET, TCA, FRAIS RETENUE A LA SOURCE, else a label with DROIT(S) DE TIMBRE, TAXE SUR VALEUR AJOUTEE, COMMISSION, FRAIS, AGIOS, INTERET; **since 21/10/2026** PRELEV / PRLV and the pointage CERTIFICATION CHEQUE are no longer fees: a prélèvement (DGI, leasing, assurance), « FINEX REMBOURSSEMENT DU MONTANT A PRELEVER » or a certified cheque's amount inflated the fees to 52 M DH over 01/09–21/10 and left the 1 → 1 engine; a real fee taken by prélèvement keeps its word, « PRELEVEMENT DES FRAIS DE DOSSIER »; `reconciliation_repository.frais_bancaire`, `POINTAGES_FRAIS`, `LIBELLE_FRAIS`), counted apart (`a_comptabiliser`, `total_a_comptabiliser`; **since 30/10/2026 they also stay in `par_statut["Non rapprochée"]` and in the « Non rapprochée » list until they are reconciled**, « À comptabiliser » being a part of « Non rapprochées »; before they were removed from it); a label starting with VIREMENT COMMERCIAL or REMISE DOCUMENTAIRE is **never** a fee, whatever its pointage (decision of 13/10/2026, `LIBELLE_PAS_FRAIS`, the label wins over the pointage: 4 VIREMENT COMMERCIAL EMIS pointed INTERET / FRAIS and 1 REMISE DOCUMENTAIRE IMPORT pointed INTERET went back to « Non rapprochée »; AGIOS BANCAIRES / COMMISSION BANCAIRE refunds on the credit side stay « À comptabiliser »), listed with `/transactions?a_comptabiliser=true`; the engine still compares them, so a fee booked in Sage gets its proposal and leaves the view. What follows describes the feature as built.

**Comptes de charge du plan comptable** (decision of 05/11/2026, no migration; completes the 16/10/2026 accounts of a manual entry): the business gave its list of 21 fee and interest accounts, `frais_service.COMPTES_FRAIS` (numéro, intitulé, banque réservée): 61473000 FRAIS COMMISSIONS BANCAIRE, 63110001 INTERET CREDOC, 63110002 INTERET RELANCE, 63110004 INTERET CMT / OXYGENE, 63111000 AGIOS B.M.C.E, 63112000 AGIOS BP, 63113000 AGIOS AWB, 63115200 INTERET DE RETARD/ OP FINANCIERE, 63116000 AGIOS BMCI, 63116300 AGIOS CIH, 63117000 FRAIS D'ESCOMPTE, 63118000 INTERETS CREDIT ENLEVEMENT, 63119400 INTERET CRED SPOT, 63119410 INTERET ACNE/MCNE, 63119420 INTERETS CREDIT AFFACTURAGE, 63119500 FRAIS DE REFINANCEMENT, 63119700 INTERET CMT, 63119710 INTERETS CMT BP 10.728 KMAD (**BP only**), 63119800 INTERETS EMPRUNTS GROUPE, 63119901 COMPTE D'ATTENTE / INTERETS, 63180000 CHARGES D'INTERET EXER ANTERIEURS (all kept, the last three included, business choice); 61671 (timbres), 61714000 (COM) and 34552142 (TVA) stay as before. `compte_charge` of a manual entry: AGIOS → the bank's account, then `compte_du_libelle` (the label starts with an intitulé, punctuation and accents ignored, INTERETS = INTERET, the longest intitulé wins: « INTERET CMT / OXYGENE » before « INTERET CMT »; 63119710 only for BP), then COM / COMMISSION 61714000, else 61473000 (« INTERETS DEBITEURS » alone stays there). A not-yet-exported manual entry follows the new rule (lines are computed when read). `GET /api/frais/comptes` (`reconciliation.view`) lists them with `banque`; `POST /api/frais/manuelle` takes an optional `compte`, one of the bank's list (409 otherwise); when it differs from the label's account, the lines are saved in `frais_lignes` (the VAT stays on 34552142; the entry then shows « modifiée »); audit `comptabilisation_manuelle` records `compte_charge`. Screen: `ManualFeeModal` has a « Compte de charge » select that follows the label (`compteDuLibelle` in `lib/frais.ts`, mirror of the server rule) and can be changed; the label suggestions add the intitulés (`suggestionsLibelles`); `EditFraisModal` suggests the bank's accounts and 34552142 on each line (free entry kept).

**P12 (built, spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-06-ecarts-design.md`)**: discrepancies on `/ecarts` (KPI À traiter · En cours · Clôturés · Montant total, filters, table, side panel with history) and « Signaler un écart » in the `/rapprochement` center panel. Each type has required lines (`discrepancy_service.LIGNES_PAR_TYPE`, mirrored in `frontend/lib/discrepancies.ts`): Banque sans écriture = a transaction; Écriture sans banque = an entry; Montant différent / Date différente = both, same company and account (Montant différent refused when the amounts balance); Libellé ambigu = a transaction, entry optional; Doublon potentiel = exactly one line. Montant = |amount| of the transaction (else the entry), différence = tx.montant + entry.montant. Creating an écart rejects pending proposals of its lines (`reconciliation_service.release_pending_matches`), a validated match gives 409.

**Generation is on demand** (`POST /api/discrepancies/generate`, decision of 06/10/2026): doublons potentiels (same account, date, amount, label; entries also same pièce; the reconciled or oldest line is the original) and lines still « Non rapprochée » dated at least `fenetre_jours` (10) before today; a line that ever had an écart is never flagged again automatically. Lifecycle À traiter → En cours → Traité (→ En cours), closure from any open status with a mandatory comment (`POST /{id}/close`), closed is final. While open, its lines are « Écart » (ignored by the engine); on closure they go back to « Non rapprochée ». Migration 0012: one open écart per transaction and per entry (partial unique indexes). Open amounts are totalled **per currency**. P11 change: validating a match whose amounts do not balance is refused (409, « Montant différent »). Reads need `reconciliation.view`, writes `discrepancies.manage`; every action locks the company row and is audited (`creation_ecart`, `generation_ecarts`, `modification_ecart`, `cloture_ecart`), and the detail's history is read from `audit_logs`.

# Position bancaire (Banques, Prévisions, Devises, graphique)

> Texte déplacé tel quel depuis `CLAUDE.md` le 04/11/2026, dans l'ordre d'origine (en anglais, comme ce fichier).
> Un paragraphe plus récent peut en remplacer un plus ancien : chercher « replaces » / « since ».

`/position-bancaire` shows the Devises and Prévisions tables **entered by hand**, cell by cell, per company and date (`GET`/`PUT /api/position/devises` and `/api/position/previsions`, tables `saisies_*`, migration 0004): a stop-gap until P9/P14 compute them, so nothing in them is calculated (not even TOTAL).

**Page order and Prévisions lines** (decision of 30/10/2026, migration 0044): the page shows Banques → Prévisions → Devises → « Évolution de la position » (the chart left `BanquesTable`, which hands its response to `PositionView` through `onCharge`; still no second API call). Each Prévisions line has **its own date**: `saisies_previsions.jour` / `saisies_previsions_jour.jour` = the line's date and `ligne` = its rank among that date's lines (CHECK `ligne_du_bloc` now 1–99, `enums.LIGNES_PREVISIONS_PAR_DATE`; existing lines kept the day they were entered as their date, no data moved; the downgrade refuses to lose lines beyond the 14th). `GET /api/position/previsions?date=` returns the lines dated that day **or later**, sorted by date then rank, never an empty line (no fixed 14-line block any more); `PUT` takes every line shown, each with its `jour` and without `ligne` (422 otherwise), and replaces all the company's lines dated from that day on in one transaction (`saisie_service.save_previsions`, `_numeroter`): a missing line is deleted, an entirely empty one is ignored, ranks are given in sending order per date, older lines are never touched; a line before the page date or before `PREMIERE_DATE_SOLDE`, or a 100th line on one date, gives 409; audit `saisie_previsions` with `a_partir_du` and cells labelled « JJ/MM/AAAA · Ligne n · … ». Screen (`PrevisionsTable`, permission `forecasts.manage`): lines grouped by date (date cell merged per group, with « + ligne » to add a line at that date), a bin per line, « Date de la ligne » + « Ajouter une ligne » (that day or later) under the table; additions and deletions apply with « Enregistrer » (« Annuler » restores); **formulas in amount cells** (30/10/2026, screen only): a cell starting with « = » (`=1 200,50+350*2`, + − * / and parentheses) is replaced by its result on Entrée or when leaving the cell (`lib/formule.ts` `evaluerFormule`, exact BigInt fractions, rounded half-up to the cent, no `eval`; `GridCell` prop `formule`, Prévisions amount cells only); only the result is saved, a wrong formula or a division by zero stays as typed and is a « cellule à corriger »; the draft keeps each line as `id|jour` + `id|…` cells (`lignesPrevisions`, `groupesParDate`, `ajouterLigne`, `supprimerLigne` in `lib/saisies.ts`, `useSaisieGrid.changeDraft`).

**Exception, decision of 05/10/2026**: the EUR and USD rows of the Devises table are read-only and show the **balances of the company's EUR / USD current accounts, in their own currency, never converted** (`GET /api/position/devises/soldes?company_id=&date=`, `position_service.ligne_devise`, same balance rule as the Banques table, last known balance greyed); TOTAL = sum of that currency, DEPASSEMENT empty; only the Exp DH convertible row is still entered by hand (older EUR / USD hand entries stay in the database, unshown). The Devises table is not shown for a company with no active EUR, USD or DH convertible account (`affiche: false`; Tefil today). P7 (statement import) is split into P7.1 analysis (done: `POST /api/statements/import/analyse`, multipart, permission `statements.import`, writes nothing), P7.2 confirmation and storage (done: `POST /api/statements/import/confirm`, same form plus `garder_doublons` and `ecarter_erreurs`; stateless, it re-sends and re-analyses the file, then writes `import_batches`, `bank_statements`, `bank_transactions`, the bank's `column_mappings`, a `balance_checks` row and the closing day's balance with source « Relevé », in one transaction with audit), P7.3 the `/releves` screen (done: import wizard Détection des colonnes → Mapping → Validation, history `GET /api/statements?company_id=`, operations `GET /api/statements/{id}/transactions`; reading needs `statements.import` OR `reconciliation.view` via `require_any_permission`).

**P8.1 (done, spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-03-tableau-banques-design.md`)**: the Banques table of `/position-bancaire` is computed by `GET /api/position/banques?company_id=&date=` (permission `position.view`, no migration): pure `Decimal` rules in `position_service.tableau_banques`, reads in `position_repository`, orchestration in `position_banques_service`. One « facilité de caisse » row per calendar day from the first balance to min(date, today); a day without a balance reuses the bank's last known one (shown greyed, « dernier solde connu »); since 30/10/2026 only the last 3 days are visible (`JOURS_AFFICHES`) and the older ones are reached with a vertical scrollbar of the table frame, opened at the bottom (header, Taux and LIGNE in a sticky `thead`, Disponible Fc reel in a sticky `tfoot`, frame height measured in `BanquesTable`; « Afficher plus » is gone, replaced by « Afficher tous les jours » / « Réduire à 3 jours », which unfolds the whole table without scrollbar, not remembered; the PDF and the chart are unchanged). LIGNE is not historised, so past days use the current LIGNE. No balance may be dated before `position_service.PREMIERE_DATE_SOLDE` (01/01/2000): entry is refused (API and form) and the table ignores older rows, because a mistyped year (« 0026 ») would otherwise create one row per day over centuries. The Disponible Fc reel row is a second `<tbody>` of the same table, so it scrolls with it and keeps the column headers. P8.2 (detail table per account, filters) was dropped by the business on 03/10/2026.

**P8.3 (done)**: at the bottom of the page since 30/10/2026 (under the Banques card before), the « Évolution de la position » card (`components/position/PositionChart.tsx`, Recharts, the first chart library of the project) draws the TOTAL « facilité de caisse » of the last 30 days ending at the chosen date (`evolutionPoints` in `frontend/lib/position.ts`), from the same `/api/position/banques` response, without a second call; a day without TOTAL is a gap, never 0. After adding a frontend dependency, also run `docker compose exec frontend npm install`: the container keeps its own `node_modules` in a named volume.

**Prévisions line dated before the page date** (decision of 09/11/2026, no migration; replaces « a line before the page date … gives 409 » of 30/10/2026): « Date de la ligne » accepts any date (no `min`, the « doit être le … ou plus tard » message is gone). In `PUT /api/position/previsions`, a line dated before the page date is **added** at its date, after the lines already saved that day (`saisie_repository.dernier_rang_previsions`, max rank over `saisies_previsions` and `saisies_previsions_jour`; `_numeroter(lignes, rangs_existants)`): nothing before the page date is replaced or deleted, the 99-lines-per-date limit counts the saved lines, and dates before `PREMIERE_DATE_SOLDE` still give 409. Audit as usual (« 08/11/2026 · Ligne 3 · libellé »). The table still shows the lines from the page date on, so the added line disappears after « Enregistrer »: the toast says « Ligne du JJ/MM/AAAA ajoutée : choisissez le JJ/MM/AAAA en haut de page pour la voir » (`PrevisionsTable.save`). Tests `test_line_before_the_chosen_date_is_added_after_that_dates_lines`, `test_line_before_the_chosen_date_respects_99_lines`.

**A balance opens its statement line** (decision of 09/11/2026, no migration): on the « facilité de caisse » rows of the Banques table, a balance that comes from a statement is a link to its line in `/releves`. API: each cell of `GET /api/position/banques` gets `comptes` [{bank_account_id, numero, solde, date_solde, operation_id, date_operation, solde_operation}] (`position_service.CompteCellule`, `comptes_cellule`; `CompteColonne` now carries `bank_account_id` and `operations` = (day, balance, id) of the last operation of each day, read by `position_repository.last_operations`, which `last_operation_soldes` now wraps). The operation is the last one dated on the row's day or before, so a greyed day (« dernier solde connu ») opens the line of the real date; a hand-entered balance has `operation_id` null and is not a link; `comptes` is empty on the Disponible Fc reel row and in the Devises table. No figure changes. Screen (`BanquesTable` `AmountCell`): one account → the amount is a link `/releves?compte=&date=&ligne=` (`lib/statements.ts` `lienReleve`); several accounts → the amount opens a small menu listing each account (« …4521 » and its own balance), each linking to its own line, a hand-entered one greyed; the link is shown only to users who can open `/releves` (`canAccess`). When pending operations from a separate file (20/10/2026) make the cell differ from the line's balance, the link's tooltip says « solde de la ligne X + opérations en cours ».

**A Prévisions formula is kept with its result** (decision of 10/11/2026, migration 0049; replaces « only the result is saved » of the 30/10/2026 formulas): an amount cell entered as a formula (`=1000+500+250`) shows its result as before, but the formula is stored next to it so the user can come back, see the detail and change or remove a number. Tables: `saisies_previsions.formule` and `saisies_previsions_jour.formule_encaissement` / `formule_escompte` / `formule_douane` (`VARCHAR(200)`, null for a plain amount; CHECK `formule_avec_montant` / `formules_avec_montants`: never a formula without its amount). The amount stays the only value anything reads. API: `banques[]` items of `GET`/`PUT /api/position/previsions` carry `formule`, each line carries `formule_encaissement` / `formule_escompte` / `formule_douane`; the server re-evaluates every formula (`formule_service.evaluer`, `Fraction`, same grammar and rounding as `lib/formule.ts`) and refuses with 409 « Formule invalide ou différente du montant (JJ/MM/AAAA, CIH) : … » one that is wrong or does not give its amount. Audit `saisie_previsions`: a bank cell with a formula is `{montant, formule}` (a plain amount stays a plain value), the day columns add « Encaissement (formule) »… Screen (`GridCell` props `formuleSaisie` / `onFormuleChange`, draft key `id|colonne|=` from `lib/saisies.ts` `formuleKey`): a cell with a formula shows a small Σ and the tooltip « Calcul : 1000 + 500 + 250 » (`detailFormule`, also read-only); clicking in it puts the formula back in the field, Entrée or leaving recalculates; typing a plain number (or emptying the cell) drops the formula. Formulas typed before 10/11/2026 were not kept: those cells stay plain amounts.

**Prévisions : toutes les lignes, quel que soit le jour choisi** (décision du 13/11/2026, sans migration ; remplace « `GET` returns the lines dated that day or later » et « `PUT` replaces all the company's lines dated from that day on » du 30/10/2026, ainsi que l'ajout à la suite d'une ligne passée et le message « choisissez le JJ/MM/AAAA » du 09/11/2026). Le tableau Prévisions montre **toutes** les lignes de la société, les plus anciennes en haut ; le jour choisi en haut de page ne les masque plus. `GET /api/position/previsions` renvoie toutes les lignes (`date` ne remplit plus que `jour` dans la réponse) ; `PUT` remplace **toutes** les lignes de la société par celles envoyées (une ligne absente est supprimée, quelle que soit sa date) et numérote les rangs dans l'ordre d'envoi pour chaque date (`saisie_service.list_previsions(db, company_id)`, `save_previsions` sans `depuis`, `_numeroter` sans `rangs_existants` ; `saisie_repository.dernier_rang_previsions` supprimée). Toujours 409 : date avant `PREMIERE_DATE_SOLDE`, 100e ligne d'une date, formule fausse. Audit `saisie_previsions` inchangé, `a_partir_du` vaut désormais 2000-01-01. Écran : toutes les lignes restent modifiables, le message après « Enregistrer » est « Tableau Prévisions enregistré. », le tableau vide dit « Aucune prévision. ». Risque connu : deux personnes qui enregistrent en même temps, la dernière remplace tout le tableau.
